The club

Football from the heart of Namayumba

BUWASA Royal Saints FC, founded in 2026, brings together players from BUWASA and the surrounding villages of Namayumba in Wakiso District. We train hard, play fair and represent our community on every pitch we step onto.

Whether you are a seasoned striker, a young talent looking for a first team, or a supporter who never misses a match, there is a place for you with the Saints.
